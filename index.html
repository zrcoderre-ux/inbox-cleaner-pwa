Suggested blocks 0

Senders you keep marking as read without ever opening. Block sends their mail to spam; Auto-read marks it read but leaves it in your inbox.

Suggested subject rules 0

Recurring subjects you never open, from senders whose other mail you do read. Subjects that vary a little each time are grouped, and the rule matches on the words they share. Auto-read marks future matching mail read and leaves it in your inbox — there's no block option here, because the sender isn't the problem.

Suggested short-mail rules 0

Senders whose mail is usually long but who sometimes send something far shorter — a “we’re live” note, a post cut off after two paragraphs, a one-line alert. Auto-read marks only those short ones read and leaves the rest of the sender’s mail exactly where it is. Lengths are measured from the emails already saved for offline reading, so nothing extra is fetched.

Rules 0

Applied to new mail every time you load. Remove a rule with × — mail already actioned stays where it is.

Add a body rule

Matches on what the email says rather than who sent it. Give it a phrase, a word limit, or both — with both, an email has to satisfy the two. A word limit on its own catches every short email, replies from people included, so it's usually worth pairing with a phrase.
